## Translated Customer Speak to Dev Speak

Translating customer reports into actionable engineering escalations was the core of my daily work at Axonius. Most customers don't provide enough detail to identify which problem they're actually reporting. A customer said "the Microsoft fetch is failing" but Axonius has around a dozen Microsoft adapters. I connected to their environment and found they were running four of them, with two showing failed fetches. I had to ask which one they meant. The answer was both, so I asked them to open separate tickets, one per adapter, to keep troubleshooting clean and make sure anyone reading the case history, including engineering if it escalated, had a clear record. By the time anything reached engineering from me, it was scoped, documented, and actionable.
