## Triage

With 50+ active tickets, you can't work them in the order they arrive. I triage on three axes: technical complexity, account politics (a customer up for renewal gets more scrutiny even on small issues), and, when two tickets are otherwise tied, contract value. I pull Salesforce to see which account carries more revenue and let that break the tie.

I also don't work one ticket to completion before starting the next. I round-robin: work a ticket until it hits a natural stopping point (waiting on the customer, waiting on engineering) and move to the next one that's actionable. On anything high severity or high urgency, I loop in the TAM and sales team early so nobody's caught off guard.
